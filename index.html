FAQ

Questions players ask

What's the best GAA strength and conditioning programme?

One built around GAA's actual demands: repeated sprints, contact, and changes of direction, not a generic gym split. The Complete GAA Off-Season Programme combines strength, speed training and injury-resilience work in one 8-week block, built for the off-season.

How do I prevent hamstring and groin injuries in GAA?

Regular eccentric hamstring work (like Nordic curls) and adductor/groin strength training are the best-evidenced ways to lower re-injury risk. Every programme includes this as standard — see our injury-risk reduction approach.

Do I need a gym?

Yes. The Complete GAA Off-Season Programme is built around gym training. If you're not sure you have the right access, get in touch before you buy.

Can I do GAA speed and strength training in-season?

The Complete GAA Off-Season Programme is built for the off-season block. If you want ongoing, in-season support, 1:1 Online or Hybrid Coaching is phased around your matches and club training.

Is it suitable for younger players?

No — the Complete GAA Off-Season Programme is for adult players (18+) only. Younger players should train under the guidance of a qualified coach and their parent or guardian.

I've had an injury before. Can I still follow it?

Injury history varies a lot. If you're carrying or returning from an injury, check with your physio or doctor before starting any programme.

How do I get access after paying?

You'll get an email straight away with your TeamBuildr login and your programme already assigned. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact us.

Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Yes. Checkout is handled by Stripe, which supports card, Apple Pay and Google Pay automatically depending on your device and browser.
