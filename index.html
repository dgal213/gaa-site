Injury prevention

Hamstrings. Groin. ACL.
Trained, not just hoped for.

Hamstring strains and groin injuries are the most common in Gaelic games, and ACL tears are among the most serious. Injury-resilience work is programmed into every block, not treated as an extra.

Hamstring

Nordic curls & eccentric strength

The single most commonly injured muscle group in GAA. Regular eccentric hamstring work is one of the best-evidenced ways to cut re-injury risk.

Groin

Adductor strength & control

Copenhagen planks and adductor strength work for the cutting, kicking and striking that load the groin hardest.

Knee / ACL

Landing & deceleration mechanics

Controlled landing, cutting and deceleration drills to build the control that protects the knee under match speed.

FAQ

Questions players ask

What's the best GAA strength and conditioning programme?

One built around GAA's actual demands: repeated sprints, contact, and changes of direction, not a generic gym split. The Complete GAA Season plan combines strength, speed training and injury-resilience work in one 12-week block, phased for the GAA calendar.

How do I prevent hamstring and groin injuries in GAA?

Regular eccentric hamstring work (like Nordic curls) and adductor/groin strength training are the best-evidenced ways to lower re-injury risk. Every programme includes this as standard — see our injury prevention approach.

Do I need a gym?

No. The programmes come with gym and minimal-equipment versions so you can train wherever you can.

Can I do GAA speed and strength training in-season?

Yes. The Season Plan is phased so in-season weeks are lighter and built around your matches and club training.

Is it suitable for younger players?

Programmes are aimed at adult and older teenage players. Younger players should train under the guidance of a qualified coach and their parent or guardian.

I've had an injury before. Can I still follow it?

Injury history varies a lot. If you're carrying or returning from an injury, check with your physio or doctor before starting any programme.

How do I get access after paying?

You'll get an email straight away with your TeamBuildr login and your programme already assigned. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact us.

Can I pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Yes. Checkout is handled by Stripe, which supports card, Apple Pay and Google Pay automatically depending on your device and browser.
